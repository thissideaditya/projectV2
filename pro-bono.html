Access to Justice

Pro Bono

ADA Law Chambers extends free or highly subsidised legal assistance to deserving individuals, common citizens, Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, Cost Accountants, and other professionals who need genuine legal guidance — because access to justice is a right, not a privilege.

The firm remains committed to helping those in need, one matter at a time, and to widening the circle of who can call on it for counsel.

Access to justice should never depend on the size of one's wallet.
