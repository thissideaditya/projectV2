Founder

Adv. Ashu Dalmia

Founder · Chartered Accountant & Advocate

Ashu Dalmia is the Founder of ADA Law Chambers, an Advocate, and a former Chartered Accountant with extensive experience in indirect taxation and commercial laws. He specializes in GST litigation and advisory services, as well as arbitration, contract drafting, dispute resolution, and commercial legal matters. He has also been actively involved in the implementation of VAT projects in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, providing strategic tax advisory and compliance support.

He has served as a Special Invitee to the Indirect Taxes Committee of ICAI, contributing significantly to the development, interpretation, and dissemination of indirect tax laws and best practices. As a distinguished faculty member of ICAI and other leading professional bodies, he has delivered more than 1,000 seminars, workshops, and training programs for professionals, corporates, government departments, and industry participants.

He is also a respected author, speaker, and thought leader in the field of indirect taxation, widely recognized for his ability to simplify complex tax laws and provide practical, business-oriented solutions to organizations and professionals.
