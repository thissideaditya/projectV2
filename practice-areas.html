GST Appeals: Commissioner to Supreme Court

When an adjudication order goes against you, the appeal is built on the record. We assess the order, prepare grounds and evidence, and handle the matter at every level, from the first appeal to the Supreme Court, including pre-deposit and stay of recovery at each stage.

  • Commissioner (Appeals)
  • GST Appellate Tribunal
  • High Court
  • Supreme Court

GST Appeals: Commissioner to Supreme Court

An appeal is usually won or lost on how well the record was built below. We review the adjudication order, find the legal and factual errors, and take the matter through each appellate stage, keeping track of limitation, pre-deposit and recovery at every step.

  • Review adjudication orders and advise on the merits, the risks and whether to appeal.
  • Draft and file first appeals before the Appellate Authority under Section 107, within the limitation period.
  • Calculate and arrange the mandatory pre-deposit, and seek stay of recovery of the balance demand.
  • File applications for condonation of delay and for additional evidence where needed.
  • File and argue second appeals before the GST Appellate Tribunal under Section 112.
  • File appeals before the High Court on substantial questions of law and before the Supreme Court.
  • File writ petitions under Article 226 against jurisdictional errors, breaches of natural justice and coercive recovery.
  • Represent clients in rectification proceedings and in implementing appellate orders, including refunds of pre-deposit.

GST Audits & Investigations

How a business handles an audit or investigation often decides whether a demand follows. We help you prepare records, deal with the officers and protect your position, so that issues are resolved early and the record is ready if the matter goes further.

  • Prepare for departmental audits under Section 65, including reconciling returns, books and e-way bill data.
  • Respond to audit observations and discrepancy notices with supporting documents and legal submissions.
  • Represent clients in special audits under Section 66 and respond to the auditor’s findings.
  • Advise and assist during inspections, searches and seizures under Section 67, including on the rights of the person searched.
  • Prepare clients for statements in response to summons under Section 70 and attend proceedings with them.
  • Represent clients in investigations by DGGI and the anti-evasion wings of the Central and State GST departments.
  • Advise on voluntary payment, on closing proceedings early and on the risk of penalties and prosecution.
  • Challenge the release of seized goods, documents and provisionally attached bank accounts.
