About the Chambers

Specialised counsel, built around GST and tax law

ADA Law Chambers is a specialised law firm with deep expertise in Goods and Services Tax (GST) and direct tax matters. We provide focused, practical, and strategic legal solutions across compliance, advisory, and dispute resolution.

Tax & Regulatory

We advise and represent businesses and individuals on direct and indirect taxation, including GST, income tax and customs, and on the regulatory frameworks that shape how they operate: FEMA, the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, arbitration and the full spectrum of corporate laws.

Our tax work covers transaction structuring, classification, valuation and input tax credit questions, GST audits, investigations, search and seizure, and replies to show cause notices, through to representation before adjudicating authorities, appellate tribunals and High Courts.

On the regulatory side, we handle foreign investment and ECB compliance and compounding under FEMA, insolvency proceedings under the IBC, and arbitration arising out of commercial and tax-linked disputes.

Litigation

Sound expertise in civil and criminal litigation, supporting clients at every stage, from preventive advice and pre-litigation strategy through to trial, appeal and final resolution of the dispute.

On the civil side, we act in commercial and contractual disputes, recovery suits, injunctions, writ petitions and arbitration-related court proceedings.

On the criminal side, we represent clients in economic offences, including GST and tax prosecutions, arrest and bail matters, and white-collar investigations. Every brief is run with careful drafting, candid advice on risk and timelines, and close coordination with the client throughout.
