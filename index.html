About the Chambers

Specialised counsel, built around GST

ADA Law Chambers is a specialised law firm with deep expertise in Goods and Services Tax (GST). We provide focused, practical, and strategic legal solutions across compliance, advisory, and dispute resolution.

Litigation

ADA Law Chambers specialises in GST litigation. We represent clients before GST authorities, the GST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT), High Courts and the Supreme Court in matters arising from Show Cause Notices, assessment and adjudication orders, denial or restriction of Input Tax Credit, demands, penalties, interest, and related proceedings.

Our work covers the full dispute lifecycle: pre-litigation strategy and replies to Show Cause Notices and audit observations; representation in adjudication and appellate proceedings.

Consultancy

ADA Law Chambers provides specialised GST and tax consultancy to businesses and individuals. We advise on the interpretation and application of GST law, including classification, valuation, place of supply, Input Tax Credit eligibility, reverse charge applicability, exemptions, and the implications of circulars, notifications and judicial precedents.

Our consultancy work covers structuring of transactions and supply chains from a GST perspective, review of contracts and commercial arrangements for tax efficiency and compliance, assistance in registration and amendment processes, and guidance on return-related and procedural issues. We also support clients in evaluating the GST impact of business decisions, expansions, reorganisations and cross-border supplies.

We further assist with opinion work on complex or contentious issues, preparation of representations and clarifications before tax authorities, and ongoing advisory support to help clients align their operations with the evolving GST framework while managing risk and optimising legitimate tax positions.
