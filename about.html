About the Chambers

Grounded in tax law. Committed to people.

ADA Law Chambers is a multidisciplinary legal practice with deep expertise in Goods and Services Tax and direct taxation, while also handling corporate, commercial, economic, regulatory, and dispute-resolution matters. With more than two decades of founding practice, the Chambers combines deep technical knowledge with practical insight to deliver clear and effective legal solutions for Indian and international clients.

Our work spans the full spectrum of advisory, compliance, structuring, documentation, and advocacy — from preventive guidance and transaction structuring through to representation before departmental authorities, appellate forums, tribunals, and courts. The firm maintains a consistent standard of evidence-led, client-focused counsel irrespective of the size or nature of the matter.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

Four principles that govern every engagement — from the largest corporate mandate to the smallest individual matter. These are not aspirational values; they are the operating standard by which every member of the Chambers is held.

Precision

Rigorous, evidence-led advisory that stands up to scrutiny in filings, audits, and courtrooms. Every submission, every reply, every argument is prepared to the standard required for the most demanding forum.

Discretion

Confidential handling of sensitive tax, corporate, economic, and personal matters. Clients share information that is commercially and personally significant; that trust is treated as absolute.

Integrity

Every client's cause is treated with the same conscience, regardless of matter size. The Chambers does not distinguish between a multinational and an individual — the standard of care is identical.

Access to Justice

Free or subsidised counsel extended to individuals and professionals who need it most. Quality legal support should not be limited by financial capacity.
